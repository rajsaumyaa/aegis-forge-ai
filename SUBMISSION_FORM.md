# CyberForge Hackathon — Project Submission Details

### 1. Project Title
**AegisForge AI**

### 2. Tagline
Autonomous AI SOC Incident Triage & IRDAI-Compliant Cyber Insurance Risk Underwriting Engine

### 3. Track / Theme
AI in Cybersecurity — Full-Stack Cyber Resilience, Autonomous Defence & Incident Triage

### 4. Problem Statement
Security Operations Centers (SOCs) are overwhelmed by millions of alerts daily, leading to analyst burnout, delayed containment (high MTTR), and vulnerability to severe breaches. Simultaneously, the cyber insurance market relies on outdated, static risk questionnaires that fail to mirror an organization's real-time security posture. Under the new **India DPDP Act 2023** (penalties up to ₹250 Cr) and **CERT-In 6-Hour SLA Reporting Mandates**, organizations urgently need continuous, automated risk quantification and containment.

### 5. Solution & Architecture
AegisForge AI is an AI-native cyber resilience console directly inspired by Mitigata's AI SOC and IRDAI insurance brokerage model:
1. **Sub-second AI Telemetry Corroboration:** Ingests and correlates raw signals across EDR (CrowdStrike), CloudTrail, and API gateways to synthesize high-fidelity incidents in <50ms.
2. **MITRE ATT&CK Mapping & Blast Radius Attribution:** Automatically identifies tactics (T1486, T1059, OWASP API1) and root cause.
3. **Dynamic IRDAI Cyber Insurance Underwriting:** Algorithmic engine that maps live security posture to financial Value at Risk (VaR) in INR and calculates dynamic policyholder premium discounts (up to 35% savings).
4. **1-Click / Auto-Pilot Autonomous Containment:** Microsegments infected hosts, kills malicious PIDs, and invalidates compromised tokens.
5. **Regulatory AI Copilot:** Generates statutory incident reports compliant with CERT-In Section 70B and DPDP Act 2023.
6. **Cryptographic Proof of Audit:** Exports Board-ready and Insurer-ready forensic certificates with SHA-256 hashes.

### 6. Alignment with Mitigata
- **AI-Powered SOC:** Mirrors Mitigata's 1M+ incident triage scale with automated correlation and playbook execution.
- **IRDAI Brokerage Synergy:** Connects real-time security telemetry directly to dynamic insurance underwriting, lowering loss ratios for insurers and rewarding resilient enterprises.
- **India Regulatory Focus:** Native support for CERT-In 6-hour SLA and India DPDP Act 2023 compliance.

### 7. Tech Stack
- **Languages:** JavaScript (ES2024), HTML5, Canvas 2D
- **Styling:** Custom Glassmorphic Cyber-Defense CSS System

- **Tooling:** Node.js, Vite 8.3
